The exhibition model runs on preset data, not live sensors. Every value is marked published (HMWSSB, GHMC, TGPCB, HMDA and studies) or preset (a demo value used by the model). This is a school project, not an official flood warning.

What happens in a heavy storm?

Pick a storm and press Run. Each 10-minute step uses the water-balance rule: change in volume = (inflow − outflow) × time. Lakes start at their preset levels.

Substations on the interconnections

One substation sits in the middle of each link (section formula, ratio 1 : 1). It measures the water passing, and it works the gate: it opens only when water can flow downhill and the water is clean enough to send on.

Get K-SWIFT as an app
