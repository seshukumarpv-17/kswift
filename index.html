The five lakes on a coordinate grid

Origin (0, 0) is 17.30°N, 78.30°E and one square is 1 km. Dashed lines are straight-line distances worked out with the distance formula. Click a lake for its details.

Distance between every pair (km)

d = √[(x₂ − x₁)² + (y₂ − y₁)²]. Real nala routes are longer than these straight lines. The Musi and Esi lines are drawn only to show direction.

What happens in a heavy storm?

Pick a storm and press Run. Each 10-minute step uses the water-balance rule: change in volume = (inflow − outflow) × time.

Catchments: Osman Sagar 738 km², Himayat Sagar 689 km², Hussain Sagar 240 km² (published). Durgam Cheruvu and Saroornagar have no published catchment, so the simulator assumes 10 × lake area – a classroom assumption.

Connect the exhibition model

USB from the ESP32

Plug the ESP32 into this laptop and press Connect. The ESP32 must be running the K-SWIFT firmware (firmware/kswift_esp32). USB works in Chrome or Edge on a laptop, not on phones.

Test without hardware
